Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TallyRelay handles information when you use the app. The app is designed to keep game and player data on your device and does not require an account.

Information We Do Not Sell

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not operate a server that receives your match history, player names, scores, avatars, or app settings. The app may collect limited crash and diagnostic information through Firebase Crashlytics as described below.

Information Stored on Your Device

TallyRelay may store the following information locally on your device so the app can provide its core features:

  • Player names and player settings
  • Scores, match state, saved matches, and game room information
  • Player avatars selected or created by you
  • App preferences and premium entitlement status

This information remains on your device unless you choose to remove it, delete the app, or use a device feature such as backup or device transfer.

Camera and Photo Library

If you choose to add a player avatar, the app may ask for access to your camera or photo library. Photos are used only for the avatar feature. We do not upload these images to our servers.

Nearby Multiplayer

If you use local multiplayer or game room features, limited player, room, score, and match information may be shared directly with nearby devices over local network, Bluetooth, or peer-to-peer connectivity to enable gameplay. This data is used for the multiplayer session and is not sent to our servers.

Motion Data

The app may use device motion data for dice or shake-based features. Motion data is used on your device for gameplay interactions and is not stored by us or sent to our servers.

Purchases and Subscriptions

If the app offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple may provide the app with purchase status information so premium features can be enabled.

Crash Reports and Diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to help identify crashes, errors, and technical issues. Crashlytics may collect diagnostic information such as crash logs, stack traces, app version, operating system version, device model, and app installation identifiers. We use this information only to improve app stability and troubleshoot technical problems.

Crash reports do not intentionally include your player names, match history, scores, avatars, or payment card details. The app currently does not use Firebase Analytics for behavioral analytics.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple services such as the App Store and StoreKit for purchases and subscription management, and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and diagnostics. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Firebase Crashlytics is provided by Google, and Google's handling of Firebase data is governed by Google's and Firebase's privacy and security terms.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not operate a server for app data, we do not knowingly receive personal information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored app data by removing saved matches, changing app settings, removing avatars, or deleting the app from your device. App Store purchase history and subscription management are handled by Apple.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
