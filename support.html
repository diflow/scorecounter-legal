Help with TallyRelay: Level Counter

TallyRelay helps your table track player levels, items, battle power, and shared dice rolls. If you need help with the app, please review the information below or contact us directly.

Contact Support

For technical issues, questions, feedback, or feature requests, email us at counter.app.support@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version
  • The version of TallyRelay you are using
  • A short description of what happened and the steps that led to it
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue

Please do not include passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information.

Multiplayer Connection Help

If players cannot join or stay connected to a game:

  • Keep the participating devices near one another.
  • Make sure Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are enabled.
  • Allow TallyRelay to access Bluetooth and the local network when prompted.
  • Confirm that all players are using the latest available version of the app.
  • Close and reopen the game room, then try connecting again.

Purchases and Premium
